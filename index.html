How it's calculated

pure alcohol (fl oz) = total fl oz × ABV
standard drinks = pure alcohol ÷ 0.6 fl oz
ABV % = proof ÷ 2

One US standard drink is 0.6 fl oz of pure alcohol: about a 12 oz beer at 5%, a 5 oz glass of wine at 12%, or a 1.5 oz shot of 80-proof liquor. Listed ABVs are typical US values. Some brands differ by state or by flavor (Four Loko, for example, is 12% in some states; a few BuzzBallz flavors run higher than 15%), so change the number when the label says something else. NIAAA's binge marker is about 4+ drinks for women or 5+ for men in roughly two hours. This tool is for education and doesn't estimate blood alcohol. Source: NIAAA — What is a standard drink?
